First time: grant camera + mic permission. In OBS: load with ?obs=1 for clean output. Add ?aur=0 to hide veils, ?eye=1 to show the (hidden-by-default) center eye, ?intensity=low for older hardware, ?bg=green for chroma key, ?test=1 to preview without webcam.
